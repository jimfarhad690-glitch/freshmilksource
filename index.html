Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about freshmilksource milk, subscriptions, and delivery.

A2 milk comes strictly from cows that produce only the A2 beta-casein protein variant (such as indigenous Gir, Sahiwal, and Red Sindhi breeds). Unlike modern Western A1 milk, which releases the peptide BCM-7 during digestion linked to discomfort and inflammation, A2 milk is easily digestible and metabolizes smoothly just like human mother's milk.
For our Raw A2 Milk, we recommend a gentle single-boil on low heat to preserve beneficial enzymes and vitamins while ensuring complete home hygiene. For our Gently Pasteurized options, you can enjoy it straight out of the bottle chilled or warm!
You have 100% control. You can pause, modify, or add extra bottles via our simple customer dashboard or via our WhatsApp concierge until 10:00 PM the night before. Unused balance is simply carried over to next month.
Every subscriber receives an insulated freshmilksource doorstep tote bag. Simply rinse your empty glass bottle with water and place it into the tote at night. When our delivery partner drops your fresh cold bottle in the morning, they swap and take the empty bottle back to our automated sanitizing facility.
Our farms are situated within a strict 60-kilometer radius of your city perimeter in lush organic rural corridors. This strict proximity limit is how we guarantee delivery under 4 hours from milking. You are always welcome to schedule a weekend farm tour with your family!
